When something is unclear, make the reasonable choice, record it and its tradeoff on the ticket's
thread, and keep going. Don't stop to wait for the human on a choice that can be changed later.

- Record: what you assumed, the alternatives, why this one, and what would make us change it.
- KISS when in doubt; YAGNI: build what the ticket needs, not what it might need.
- A decision the human has already made is not open: check the ticket's ask and thread first,
  and never override the human's own words with an assumption.

choice. KISS in times of doubt, and YAGNI - don't build too much." And (via advisor): "please make
reasonable assumptions and record tradeoffs and keep the project moving overnight" ... "I've given
a reasonable outline of the work and we can always pivot going forwards."
